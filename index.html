Building a pool in Wollongong

Wollongong's escarpment shapes almost every pool project. Western suburbs such as Farmborough Heights, Keiraville and Mount Ousley are often steep, so retaining, drainage and access for machinery or a crane are common topics. The flatter beachside strip from Corrimal to Thirroul is usually easier to dig, but blocks can be narrow.

Steep sites near the escarpment may also need geotechnical advice and careful stormwater design. Builders who work locally will know to check this before they quote.

How much does a pool cost in Wollongong?

It depends on the type, size, site, access, fencing and what's included. We don't publish our own prices. Industry guides linked in our pool cost guide give national ranges only. A local builder has to quote your block.

Do I need approval for a new pool?

Usually, yes. NSW pools need council or certifier approval (often complying development), a licensed builder, a principal certifier, and an occupation certificate before the pool is used. See our NSW pool approvals guide.

Do I have to register the pool?

Yes. All pools and spas in NSW must be registered on the NSW Swimming Pool Register.

Do you cover Figtree, Mount Ousley, Corrimal, Thirroul, Unanderra, Farmborough Heights, Keiraville and Woonona?

Yes. We take requests from across Wollongong and nearby areas. The builder confirms whether they can service your street.

What's the difference between concrete, fibreglass and plunge pools?

Concrete is built on site and can be custom shaped. Fibreglass is a pre-formed shell that is delivered and installed. A plunge pool is a smaller pool in either material. See our pool types guide.

Is there any obligation if I send the form?
