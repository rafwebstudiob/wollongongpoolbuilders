Pool types in Wollongong: concrete, fibreglass and plunge

In short: Most new backyard pools in Wollongong are concrete, fibreglass or a smaller plunge pool. You don't need to have decided before you ask for a quote.

A plain-English comparison, then a free form to pass your project to one local pool builder.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Concrete pools

A concrete pool is built on site, so the shape, depth and features can be tailored to the block. Raised spas, beach entries and freeform shapes are common reasons people choose concrete. The build usually takes longer than a fibreglass install because the shell is formed, finished and cured on site.

Fibreglass pools

A fibreglass pool arrives as a pre-formed shell. It is craned or placed into the excavated hole, then backfilled, connected and finished. Time on site is often shorter, as long as the shell fits the block and there is access to get it in. Sizes and shapes are limited to the moulds a manufacturer offers.

Plunge pools

A plunge pool is a smaller pool for compact courtyards, tighter lots or sloping sites. It can be concrete or fibreglass. Pool safety laws still apply to small pools, so don't assume a plunge pool is exempt.

Choosing for Wollongong

Wollongong has a mild coastal climate, with warm summers and the escarpment catching heavy rain at times. The city is squeezed between the Illawarra escarpment and the sea, so western suburbs are often steep, while the beachside strip is flatter. Fit to the block, the look you want, how long you can allow for construction, and what's included in the quote matter more than a general comparison. If you're unsure, choose “Not sure” on the form.
